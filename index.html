How do I add friends?

Open the Profile tab (bottom right) and tap Friends & groups. Tap Import to pick people from your contacts, or + to add someone by hand. Add a few things they like to do and when they're usually free to get better suggestions.

How do I connect my calendar?

In Profile, under Your calendar, tap the calendar you use: Google, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Proton or another one. If that account is already on your iPhone, it connects in one tap. If not, the app shows how to paste your calendar's private link.

How do I rate places I've been?

Open any place and tap "I've been here". Pick how it was, answer a couple of quick "which did you like more?" questions, then add photos, who was there and notes. Your ranked list is at the top of Profile.

Why don't I see places near me?

Tap the city button at the top of Discover and choose "Use my current location", or type a city. If location is off, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Social Butterfly.

Can my friends see my plans or calendar?

Not yet. Everything stays on your iPhone. Sharing plans with friends is coming in a future update.

How does Book it work?

Book it opens the place's booking page, like OpenTable for restaurants or a salon's own website, so you can reserve directly with them. Some booking links include a partner code, which may earn us a small commission at no cost to you.

How do I choose Apple Maps or Google Maps?

Open Profile and pick your maps app under Maps.

Privacy Policy

Social Butterfly for iPhone · Effective October 2026

The short version: Social Butterfly keeps your information on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers that store your data. We don't sell or share it, and there are no ads or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your friends (names, phone numbers, what they like to do and when they're usually free), groups, Wish List, plans, the places you've been with your ratings, notes and photos, your profile photo and your settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Contacts

If you choose to import friends, the app asks for access to your contacts so you can pick who to add. Only the people you pick are copied into the app, and only their names and phone numbers. Nothing is uploaded.

Photos

When you add a profile photo or photos of a place you've been, you pick them with Apple's photo picker, which only shares the photos you choose. They're saved on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Calendar

If you connect your calendar, the app reads it on your iPhone to see when you're busy, so suggestions skip those times and clashing plans get flagged. Your events never leave your device. When you add a plan to your calendar, it's saved to the calendar you chose on your iPhone, or the app opens your calendar's own page (such as Google Calendar or Outlook) with the plan filled in.

If you paste a private calendar link (for example, Google's "secret address in iCal format"), the app downloads that calendar directly from your calendar provider to find busy times. The link and your events are kept only on your iPhone.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to suggest places near you and to show today's weather. To do that, it sends searches (like "coffee shop" near your area) to Apple Maps, and your approximate location to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit). Apple handles these under the Apple Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store your location. You can pick a city by hand instead.

Invites, booking and links

When you invite friends, the app opens Messages with a draft for you to send. When you tap Book it, Map, a calendar link or a website, that site or app (such as OpenTable, Apple Maps, Google Maps or Google Calendar) opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Some booking links may include a partner code, so we may earn a commission when you book. This doesn't change the price you pay, and no personal information is sent to us.

Children

Social Butterfly isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds features like shared plans or accounts, we'll update this policy before that version is released and explain what changes.
